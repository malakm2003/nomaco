questions

YOU asked.

How much space does it need?

A spot roughly the size of a café table and one regular plug. Inside, or outside under cover.

Do the photos fade?

Keep them away from heat and sunny windowsills and they'll hang around. Want backups? Guests can grab the digital file too.

Can the prints be customized?

Absolutely — send us your names, colors, logo, or a hashtag and we'll lay out the strip around it.

How far in advance should I book?

The sooner the better, Saturdays fill up fast. Drop your date below and we'll let you know.
